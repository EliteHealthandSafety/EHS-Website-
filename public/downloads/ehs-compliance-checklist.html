The Scottish Business H&S Compliance Checklist

A plain-English check of the essentials every Scottish business should have in place. Tick what you've got, spot the gaps — and if anything's missing, we can help.

1 · Fire safety

Fire (Scotland) Act 2005 · Fire Safety (Scotland) Regulations 2006
  • Current fire risk assessment — suitable & sufficient, reviewed regularly and after any change.
  • Fire detection & alarms — in place, tested and maintained.
  • Extinguishers serviced — right types, in date, accessible.
  • Escape routes & signage — clear, unobstructed, well signed.
  • Staff fire awareness — everyone knows what to do and how to evacuate.

2 · First aid

Health and Safety (First-Aid) Regulations 1981
  • First-aid needs assessment — done for your risk level and headcount.
  • Enough trained first-aiders — appropriate to the size and layout of the workplace.
  • Stocked, in-date first-aid kit(s) — and someone responsible for them.
  • Accident recording — accident book kept; RIDDOR-reportable events understood.

3 · Policies & documents

Health and Safety at Work etc. Act 1974
  • Written H&S policy — a legal requirement once you have 5 or more employees.
  • Risk assessments — for your significant hazards, written down and up to date.
  • COSHH assessments — where hazardous substances are used or stored.
  • Employers' liability insurance — in place and certificate displayed/available.

4 · Training & competence

  • Inductions — new starters receive H&S induction for their role and site.
  • Role-specific training — manual handling, IOSH/SSSTS, asbestos awareness, work at height as relevant.
  • Training records — kept, with refresher dates tracked.

5 · Ongoing management

  • Named responsible person — someone owns H&S day to day.
  • Review dates set — assessments and documents have a review schedule.
  • Contractors managed — CDM duties met where you carry out construction work.
