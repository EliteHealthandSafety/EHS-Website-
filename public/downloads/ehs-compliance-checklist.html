The Scottish Business H&S Compliance Checklist

A plain-English check of the essentials every Scottish business should have in place. Tick what you've got, spot the gaps, and note when each item is due for review.

⏱Green tags show a suggested review cycle. These are best-practice guides: some intervals are risk-based rather than fixed by law, so the right frequency can vary by premises. Not sure what applies to you? That's exactly what a free consultation covers.

1 · Fire safety

Fire (Scotland) Act 2005 · Fire Safety (Scotland) Regulations 2006
  • Fire risk assessment: suitable & sufficient, kept current. ⏱ review annually & after any change
  • Fire detection & alarms: in place and maintained. ⏱ user test weekly · service 6-monthly
  • Emergency lighting: working on escape routes. ⏱ monthly flick test · annual full test
  • Fire extinguishers: right types, accessible. ⏱ service annually · extended 5-yearly
  • Escape routes & signage: clear and well signed. ⏱ check ongoing
  • Staff fire awareness: everyone knows what to do. ⏱ refresh ~annually

2 · First aid

Health and Safety (First-Aid) Regulations 1981
  • First-aid needs assessment: for your risk level & headcount. ⏱ review after any change
  • Enough trained first-aiders: for the size & layout of the workplace. ⏱ requalify every 3 years
  • Stocked, in-date first-aid kit(s): with someone responsible. ⏱ check monthly
  • Accident recording: accident book kept; RIDDOR understood. ⏱ RIDDOR: report within legal timescales

3 · Policies & documents

Health and Safety at Work etc. Act 1974
  • Written H&S policy: legally required at 5+ employees. ⏱ review annually
  • Risk assessments: for your significant hazards. ⏱ review annually & on change
  • COSHH assessments: where hazardous substances are used. ⏱ review on change of substance/process
  • Legionella risk assessment: for your water systems (commonly missed). ⏱ review ~every 2 years
  • Employers' liability insurance: in place and certificate available. ⏱ renew annually

4 · Training & competence

  • Inductions: H&S induction for role & site. ⏱ on joining
  • Role-specific training: manual handling, IOSH/SSSTS, asbestos awareness, work at height. ⏱ refresh every 1 to 3 years
  • Training records: kept, with refresher dates tracked. ⏱ keep current

5 · Ongoing management

  • Named responsible person: someone owns H&S day to day. ⏱ ongoing
  • Review dates diarised: so nothing lapses. ⏱ set reminders
  • Contractors managed: CDM duties met for construction work. ⏱ per project
