**What is a Health and Safety Policy?**

A health and safety policy is a formal document that outlines your company’s approach to protecting employees, contractors, and visitors from harm. It demonstrates your commitment to preventing accidents, promoting well-being, and managing risks effectively.

Under UK law, businesses with five or more employees must have a written health and safety policy. But even smaller companies benefit from having one in place.

**Core Components of a Health and Safety Policy**

At Elite Health and Safety Limited, we help clients develop policies that cover these three essential sections:

- ** Statement of Intent**

A declaration of your company’s commitment to health and safety, signed by the most senior person in the organization. This sets the tone for a safety-first culture.

- ** Responsibilities**

This section defines who is responsible for implementing and managing various aspects of health and safety—from directors and managers to supervisors and frontline staff.

- ** Arrangements**

This is where you outline the specific procedures and systems in place. It includes:

- Risk assessments

- Employee training programs

- Emergency procedures

- Reporting systems

- Equipment maintenance protocols
