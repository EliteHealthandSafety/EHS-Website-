## CITB Health and Safety Awareness Course in Glasgow

The CITB Health and Safety Awareness course provides essential safety knowledge for anyone entering the construction industry. In Glasgow, this one‑day course is widely used by labourers and apprentices seeking their CSCS Labourer Card, as it covers the fundamental principles of staying safe on site.

Participants learn about common hazards, legal responsibilities, site rules, PPE requirements, manual handling, and emergency procedures. The course also focuses on personal responsibility and the importance of good communication when working around others.

Our Glasgow‑based training team delivers the CITB Health and Safety Awareness course in a clear and practical format, ensuring delegates gain the confidence needed to work safely. At the end of the day, candidates complete a short assessment to demonstrate their understanding.

Completion of this course, combined with the CITB HS&E touchscreen test, allows delegates to apply for their CSCS Labourer Card. It is an ideal starting point for anyone beginning their construction career in Scotland.
