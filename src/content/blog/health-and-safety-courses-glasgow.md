Our Services

1. Consultancy Services

Compliance Advisory: We offer expert advisory services to clients, principal designers, and principal contractors, including the development of comprehensive pre-construction and construction phase plans to ensure adherence to all safety regulations.

Site Inspections:

Our extensive UK-wide site inspections and audits are designed to help businesses maintain compliance with safety standards, providing an extra layer of assurance for your operations.

ISO Standards Certification:

We provide guidance and support in achieving certifications such as ISO 9001, ISO 45001, and ISO 14001. Attaining these standards enhances your organization’s credibility and operational efficiency, making you more competitive in your industry.

PQQ Assistance:

Our experts assist you in completing Pre-Qualification Questionnaires (PQQ), streamlining your procurement processes and making it easier for your business to secure contracts.

Fire Risk Assessments:

We conduct thorough fire risk assessments tailored for buildings of varying complexities, ensuring compliance with fire safety regulations across the UK.

Legionella Testing and Risk Assessments:

Our specialised services target the risks associated with Legionella bacteria in water systems, helping to prevent health hazards and ensuring compliance with health regulations.

2. Training Courses Recognising that employees are the most valuable asset of any organization, we offer a wide array of externally approved Health and Safety training courses, including:

IOSH Managing Safely:

This course empowers managers with essential knowledge to effectively manage health and safety responsibilities within their teams.

IOSH Working Safely:

Designed for all employees, this course educates participants about the fundamental principles of workplace safety.

Work at Height:

Training that focuses on safe practices for conducting tasks at height, ensuring staff are well-equipped to work safely in elevated environments.

Asbestos Awareness:

This crucial training informs workers about the risks of asbestos exposure and the necessary safety measures to take.

Abrasive Wheel Safety:

We provide guidance on the correct handling and operation of abrasive wheels, minimizing the risks associated with their use.

Manual Handling:

This training covers effective techniques for safe manual lifting and handling, aimed at preventing injuries.

SSSTS (Site Supervisor Safety Training Scheme):

This course prepares site supervisors with the critical health and safety knowledge needed to lead their teams safely.

First Aid Courses:

We offer comprehensive first aid training, with options for 1, 2, and 3-day courses designed to equip staff with life-saving skills. Our training sessions can be delivered on-site at your location or at our facilities, catering to both individual staff members and larger groups.
