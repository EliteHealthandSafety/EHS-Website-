## CITB SSSTS Training Course in Glasgow

The CITB Site Supervisor Safety Training Scheme (SSSTS) is an essential qualification for anyone managing teams on construction sites. Our SSSTS courses in Glasgow provide supervisors with the knowledge they need to lead safely and meet legal responsibilities.

The course covers risk assessment supervision, method statements, toolbox talks, accident reporting, behavioural safety, and effective site communication. It gives supervisors the skills required to identify hazards early and maintain safe working conditions.

Delivered by experienced trainers, our Glasgow SSSTS courses are interactive and practical. Delegates complete an assessment at the end of the course and receive a CITB‑accredited certificate upon passing.

This training is ideal for new or existing supervisors looking to strengthen their safety leadership and support overall site compliance.

#### Assessment Overview

### SSSTS Course Assessment

The assessment at the end of the SSSTS course is designed to evaluate the delegates’ understanding and application of key safety principles. It consists of 25 questions, including multiple-choice and short written responses. Among these, four questions are safety critical, requiring detailed answers. Each question carries one point, with safety critical questions valued between 1 to 3 points. To pass, delegates must correctly answer all safety critical questions and achieve an overall score of at least 80%, equivalent to 24 out of 30 correct answers.

Delegates will be tested on their knowledge of legal responsibilities, risk management, and effective supervision techniques. The assessment ensures that participants are well-prepared to implement safety measures on-site. Successful completion of the assessment results in the awarding of the CITB SSSTS certificate, valid for five years, demonstrating the delegate’s commitment to maintaining a safe working environment.
